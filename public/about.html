My story

September 2022 to now

Everything below happened at or around the University of Oregon, oldest first. The short version is on the home page.

  1. Started at University of Oregon

    September 2022

    Double majored in Computer Science & Data Science.

  2. Co-founded Oregon Software Consulting

    March 2023

    Oregon's first student software development club. Ran the club's first recruitment cycle and managed a team of five developers. We built software for local businesses.

  3. Tutoring Mathematics

    September 2023

    Started tutoring mathematics at the University of Oregon, my most fulfilling role to date. Would continue this till graduation.

  4. Genomics Data Analyst, Rohlfs Evolutionary Genomics Lab

    July 2024

    My first research role. Analyzed primate genome sequences studying tandem repeats, identifying markers of evolution using code.

  5. Data Engineering at Expertflow

    August 2024

    Built and optimized data pipelines. My first internship, first stand ups, first PR review, first look at legacy code (ouch). Amazing summer.

  6. Data/ML Engineering at Trimble

    July 2025

    My second internship. Spent the summer creating and deploying machine learning software and untangling legacy deployment pipelines. Learned from some amazing people, an unforgettable experience.

  7. Joined the AI Club

    September 2025

    Joined my university's AI Club. Created software for all kinds of clients, from regional businesses to Fortune 100 enterprises. The first time I had deployed software at scale, across multiple timezones. Met some amazing people here, and made memories that will last a lifetime.

  8. NexHacks '26

    January 2026

    First hackathon win! Flew out to Carnegie Mellon University and built Steward AI, a desktop application that lets businesses understand their AI usage in the workplace. The amount of caffeine consumed during this probably took a couple years off my life...but worth the memories!

  9. Co-founder at Unpak

    January 2026

    Created a startup with some friends! Built a piece of software that mapped workflows at a company, and improved them using AI. Late nights, early mornings, late nights that turned into early mornings (startups are awesome).

  10. Graduated

    June 2026

    Bachelors of Science, Computer Science & Data Science, University of Oregon. Now looking for the next cool idea to build.
