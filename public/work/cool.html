  • Liquid Glass

    The navigation lens from the Unpak website, pulled out on its own. A WebGL pill of glass slides between the tabs — hover the row and it tracks the cursor, shrinking as it runs out of tabs; click one and it springs over and settles.

    There is no blur filter or backdrop trick behind it. The tab row is drawn once into a texture, and a fragment shader renders the pill from a signed-distance field: the edge band bends the background by Snell's law with a slightly different index per colour channel, which is where the chromatic fringing comes from, and fresnel and glare terms computed in LCH colour space put the light on the rim. Two springs drive the centre and the width, so the glass stretches with its own velocity instead of teleporting.

    Flip the toggle to slow motion to watch the springs work.

    Technical specifications

    What it is
    A refractive lens over a tab row: SDF pill, Snell-law edge refraction with per-channel dispersion, LCH fresnel and glare, spring-driven motion.
    Built on
    WebGL 2 and vanilla JavaScript, no libraries. Falls back to a plain CSS bubble where WebGL 2 isn't available.
    Origin
    Written for the Unpak website's navigation; this page runs the same shader, unminified and annotated.
