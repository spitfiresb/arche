AI strategy

Finding AI opportunities is easy. Knowing which are worth building is not, and it is where most AI budgets are wasted. unpak brings the evidence and the experience to make that call, then turns the winners into a plan your team can actually build.

See the approach↓

Know what's worth it

unpak surfaces every place AI could help. The harder question is which of those are actually worth building, and answering it takes judgment.

Experience

We have put AI into real workflows before, so we know which ideas pay off and which quietly stall after the demo.

Fit to your data

An opportunity only counts if it works with how your data and systems actually run, not just in a generic demo.

Worth the cost

Plenty of AI solutions work and still are not worth it. We weigh effort against payoff so you build the ones that earn it.

A build-ready plan

The opportunities worth pursuing become a sequenced roadmap, with a spec for each one detailed enough to hand straight to an engineer or a vendor. Every item lands in one portfolio you can track from proposed to shipped.

Whatever we build plugs into the systems your work already runs on.

AI costs less than you think

The price of running AI has fallen faster than most budgets have noticed. Today's models cost a fraction of what they did a couple of years ago, and used well, a focused solution runs for far less than the work it replaces.

The models are cheap

Modern models cost pennies per task. Most of the old sticker shock is simply out of date.

We build lean

A solution scoped to one workflow is small, so it is cheap to run and cheap to maintain.

It pays for itself

When something runs for cents and saves hours, the math is not close.

Build, buy, or partner

Not everything should be built from scratch. For each solution we make the call that gets you the result fastest: build it, buy a tool that already does it, or bring in a partner.

Build

When the work depends on your private data or logic, a purpose-built solution wins.

Buy

When a tool already does the job well, we point you to it instead of rebuilding it.

Partner

When it needs specialist hands, we bring in the right partner to deliver.
