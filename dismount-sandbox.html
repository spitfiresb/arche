Dismount — sandbox

Just the tail of the about-page climb, isolated: the last hops down the rope, the touchdown crouch and rise out of the hang (grip still on the rope), then the rope release and the first couple steps of the walk. Jump to any beat, slow it down, toggle the cliff and rope away, and edit the numbers in dismount-sandbox.html — the rig, poses and physics are the same code as about.html, in one fixed frame with no scroll / ring-cleaning / camp machinery. (The later half of the same sequence lives in camp-sandbox.html.)

Coordinate frame (same as about.html, in rope-relative space): rope plumb at x=0, ground at y=200. He grips the rope at x=0 and braces his feet on the cliff face to the right (x≈11–36); restY = floorY-57 is the grip height at which his feet meet the turf. The run-out splits the remaining drop into shrinking bounces — raise HOP_START_ABOVE for more hops. The last hop firms his feet onto the turf and holds the reclined pose until they're planted, so he never uprights in mid-air. He then keeps gripping the rope for the let-go delay (slider, or releaseAfter ms) before letting go and walking on; the whole arm swings from the shoulder (swingArm()).
