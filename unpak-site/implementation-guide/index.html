Implementation guide

Most enterprise rollouts are their own transformation project. unpak isn't. One install puts it to work, your teams change nothing about their day, and the first map of where AI pays off arrives within weeks. Here is the whole playbook.

See the playbook↓

The whole rollout in one install

Three steps, and only the first one involves you. Everything after the install happens on its own.

Install

One install and unpak is running. No months of integrations to wire. No connectors to configure, no rituals for your team, no lift on your IT.

Understand

unpak discovers where AI is active in your current workflows. It sees what gets repeated, where time goes, and where people get stuck, read from the work itself instead of hoping it comes up in an interview.

Surface

Within weeks, you get the map: where AI fits, what it would cost, what it would save, ranked from highest leverage down.

What it asks of your company

Here is the checklist an enterprise rollout usually comes with, and what is left of it with unpak.

Rollout checklist
  • Wire integrations and build data pipelines
  • Configure connectors, then babysit them
  • Train every team on a new tool
  • Change how people work day to day
  • Keep dashboards fed by hand
  • Deploy one app
