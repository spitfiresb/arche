Opportunities worth building

Where the hours are going

Every pattern carries the real hours behind it, so the work quietly consuming the most time across the company rises to the top.

Every recurring workflow is matched across the company, counted from real activity, and surfaced only when the evidence says AI would genuinely help.

unpak groups the same workflow wherever it shows up, across people and teams, so you can see the procedures your company runs over and over.

Every pattern is counted straight from the work: how many times it ran, across how many people, and how long each run took.

On top of the patterns, unpak points to the recurring, time-consuming steps that AI can realistically take off people's plates, and it is selective about it, so what reaches you deserves the attention.

Drill down to the proof

Every workflow, count, and hour on the platform links back to the observed activity underneath it. Start at the summary and keep stepping down until you reach the moment itself.

Follow the chain

Click from a headline number to the workflow behind it, then to the step, then to the raw moment it came from.

Counted by code

Every count and duration is computed deterministically from the capture. The models write the prose; they never do the math.

Ready for scrutiny

When a recommendation reaches the boardroom, the evidence that backs it comes along, so anyone can check the reasoning for themselves.

Executive reporting

Every workflow unpak finds becomes a short report like the one below: what the work is, how much time it takes each week, and where that time goes. It reads in a minute, and it is built from the same data as the deep analysis.
