What you'd be building

unpak reconstructs how work actually happens from observed activity, then shows companies exactly where AI will pay off. The problems underneath that are hard, unsolved, and worth solving.

Reconstruct real work

Turn streams of observed activity into honest workflow blueprints: the real steps, real time counts, real recurrence.

Make evidence decide

Every recommendation traces back to the observed moments it came from, so anyone can check the reasoning down to the raw record.

Keep the picture true

The picture lives on after deployment, confirming from real work that the AI a company shipped is actually working.
